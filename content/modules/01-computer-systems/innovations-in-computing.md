## Key Computing Innovations

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Enables computers to learn, make decisions, and perform tasks that normally require human intelligence (conversations, generating images, writing code).
- **Augmented Reality (AR) vs. Virtual Reality (VR):**
  - **AR** = Real world + virtual world (overlays digital content onto the real world).
  - **VR** = Entirely virtual environment for users to explore.
- **Metaverse:** A collective virtual shared space merging physical and digital realities via VR/AR (e.g., testing paint colours virtually in your home).
- **Social Media:** Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and X connecting billions via instant communication and smart recommendation/feed algorithms.
- **Robots:** Computer-controlled machines that sense, think, and act automatically using **sensors** (observe), **computers** (process/decide), and **motors** (move/act).
- **Quantum Computing:** Uses principles of quantum physics to solve certain problems much faster than traditional computers by processing many possibilities simultaneously rather than one at a time.
- **3D Printing:** Creates physical objects from digital designs by layering materials (plastic, metal, resin). Example: IIT Madras built India's first 3D-printed house (500 sq. ft., 21 days); Icon + Lennar partnered on a 3D-printed home community in Austin, Texas.
