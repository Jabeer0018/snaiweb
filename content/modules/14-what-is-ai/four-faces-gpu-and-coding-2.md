## Calculate vs. Predict & History

- **Calculate:** Known step-by-step rules (shopping bill). **Predict:** Uncertain patterns ("Is it a cat?").
- **1950:** Alan Turing proposes the **Turing Test** (judge behaviour via text chat).
- **1956:** John McCarthy coins **"Artificial Intelligence"** at the Dartmouth workshop.
- **2014:** "Eugene Goostman" chatbot sparks debate over passing a 5-minute Turing Test.

## The Four Faces of AI (Russell & Norvig)

| | Human-centric (mimicking us) | Rationality-centric (ideal performance) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thinking (internal)** | **Thinking Humanly** — Cognitive Science | **Thinking Rationally** — Laws of Thought |
| **Acting (external)** | **Acting Humanly** — Turing Test approach | **Acting Rationally** — **The Rational Agent** (modern AI focus) |

Modern AI focuses on **Rational Agents** because humans make mistakes and pure logic is too slow.

## Eight Disciplines & GPU Parallelism

AI grew from Philosophy, Mathematics, Economics, Neuroscience, Psychology, Computer Engineering, Control Theory, and Linguistics.

- **CPU (Sequential):** Sports car (4–8 fast cores) — great for standard code.
- **GPU (Parallel):** Fleet of buses (thousands of cores) — mirrors neural network parallelism and enabled deep learning.

## Coding 1.0 vs. Coding 2.0

| | Coding 1.0 | Coding 2.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You supply** | Step-by-step procedure (the "how") | Labelled examples/data |
| **Slogan** | You solve the problem and write the procedure | **Data becomes the program** |
